About us

Healthcare, reimagined by students

MedTech Innovators is VIT's student-run club at the crossroads of medicine, engineering and code. We bring together biomedical engineers, programmers, designers and future doctors to build technology that actually helps people.

From soldering a pulse oximeter at 2 a.m. to pitching an AI diagnosis tool to clinicians, we learn by building — together.

  • Build real medical devices & wearables
  • Solve healthcare problems with AI and data
  • Learn from doctors, researchers & founders
  • Publish, compete and take ideas to hospitals
