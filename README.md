# Welcome to MedTech Innovators · VIT! 🩺⚡

> *Where code meets care.*

Welcome to **MedTech Innovators**, the healthcare technology club of Vellore Institute of Technology, Vellore!

---

### 👋 Who We Are
We are a vibrant community of engineers, coders, designers, bioinformaticians, and future healthcare pioneers collaborating to build real-world healthcare technology solutions.

Whether you're interested in:
- 🔬 **Biomedical Hardware & Wearables** — sensors, embedded devices, and prototypes
- 🧠 **AI in Healthcare** — diagnostic algorithms, clinical computer vision, and NLP
- 🧬 **Bioinformatics** — genomic data pipelines and computational biology
- 🎨 **Design & UX** — patient-friendly interfaces and healthcare design
- 📑 **Research & Innovation** — paper discussions, ideation, and publishing

...there's a spot for you here! No prior medical background is required — just curiosity, enthusiasm, and a passion to innovate.
